Helpful answers

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers to help you get started with Zentoolo AI Resume Builder.

What is Zentoolo AI Resume Builder?

Zentoolo Resume Builder is an online resume creation tool that helps you enter career information, select a professional template, preview your resume, review content readiness, and export a PDF.

Can I download my resume as a PDF?

Yes. Use the resume builder's PDF export option to download your resume for job applications, sharing, or printing.

Who can use this resume builder?

Students, freshers, job seekers, working professionals, freelancers, career changers, and career counselors can use it to organize resume information.

Does the ATS readiness score guarantee an interview?

No. The score is a rule-based content checklist. Applicant tracking systems differ, and the checklist cannot guarantee parsing, ranking, interview invitations, or job offers.

Can I import my existing resume details?

The current builder supports structured JSON and certain labeled TXT files. Imported information should be reviewed for accuracy before exporting.

Does the builder generate resume content using AI?

The current version includes editing, templates, importing, live preview, and a readiness checklist. AI-generated summaries, job description matching, and AI rewriting require additional integration and are not currently included.
